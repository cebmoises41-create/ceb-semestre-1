Positive Sentences & Routines

📖 Present Simple (Routines)

We use the Present Simple to talk about routines, habits, and things we do every day (e.g., I wake up at 7:00 am).

⚠️ The "S" Rule (He, She, It)

When the subject is He, She, or It, you MUST add an -s or -es to the verb.

  • I work in an office. (No S)
  • You play soccer. (No S)
  • He works in an office. (+ S)
  • She plays soccer. (+ S)
  • It rains a lot. (+ S)
