📖 What is an Adjective?

An adjective is a word that describes a noun (a person, place, or thing) or a pronoun. It tells us what kind, how many, or which one.

In English, adjectives usually go before the noun (e.g., a happy dog) or after the verb 'to be' (e.g., the dog is happy).
